Pre‑Flight Questions

Frequently asked before takeoff.

The questions that come up most before and after boarding. If yours isn't here, the Mod/Admin Team is one ticket away.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I actually get in?+
Join through the invite, then complete the one‑time verification check. Once that clears, the Member role unlocks the rest of the terminal.
What roles should I grab first?+
Head to the self‑role channel and pick your gates (Social, Event, Gaming, Academy, Society) plus NotifyMember if you want pinged on announcements and events.
I got warned or muted — can I appeal it?+
Yes. Per the Rulebook (A9), an appeal system is available through designated channels and forms — don't argue the decision in public channels, since that's a rule on its own.
How do moderation actions actually escalate?+
A four‑strike system: Verbal Warning → 24‑Hour Mute → 3‑Day Ban → Permanent Ban. Severe violations or Discord ToS breaches can skip straight to a permanent ban. Every action is sent to you via DM.
Can I advertise my own server or project here?+
Not without permission — unauthorized advertising, mass DMs, and invite spam are against the rules (A5). If you want a real partnership, that's a different process — see the next question.
How do server partnerships work?+
Partnered communities get the Community Partnership role and access to the partnership‑only channel for cross‑promotion. Reach out to the Mod/Admin Team to start that conversation.
I found a bug or need help with something — who do I ask?+
Open a ticket. It routes straight to the Mod/Admin Team and gets logged, so nothing gets lost in general chat.
What's the difference between the gates and the minigames?+
Gates (Social, Event, Gaming, Academy, Society) are the main community categories. Minigames are the small, low‑effort games — fishing, counting, conquest, and more — that live in their own channels for whenever you want a break.

Still stuck?

Open a ticket once you're in, or check the Rulebook for anything conduct‑related.
