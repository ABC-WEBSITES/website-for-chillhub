Something's always boarding.

Events are how ChillHub actually feels alive — scheduled ones you can plan around, and surprise ones that just show up on the board. Here's how the program works and where to watch for the next one.

Flight Types

What kinds of events run

Events aren't limited to one gate — most tie back to a category you already know from the terminal map.

🎈

Social Meetups

Casual hangouts, movie nights, and open voice sessions in Community Hall — no agenda required.

Via · Social gate
🎮

Gaming Nights

Group sessions, tournaments, and LFG pushes for whatever the server's playing that week.

Via · Gaming gate
🎓

Academy Workshops

Study sessions and skill-sharing threads for members working on something specific.

Via · Academy gate
🌐

Society Gatherings

Club-run meetups for whichever special-interest societies are active that month.

Via · Society gate
Flight Path

How an event reaches you

Every event follows the same route from announcement to landing — no digging through channels to find out what's happening.

Stop 01
Posted in event‑announcement
Stop 02
Pinged via self‑role
Stop 03
Runs in its gate's channel
Final Stop
Recapped for anyone who missed it

Don't miss the next one

Grab the event ping role from self‑role once you're in, and keep an eye on event‑announcement.
